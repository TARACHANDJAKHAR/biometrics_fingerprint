Methodology & Architecture Overview

1. Fixed-Length Embedding Advantage

Conventional minutiae matchers perform \(O(n^3)\) or \(O(n^2)\) point-set graph alignment. DeepPrint projects the entire fingerprint into a 192-dimensional unit-norm vector (\(\mathbb{S}^{191}\)). Comparison is reduced to a single vector dot product \(s = R_p^T R_g\) executed in under 0.5 microseconds.

2. Zero Data Leakage (Open-Set)

Evaluation enforces strict subject isolation. Fingerprints from test subjects were never encountered during backbone warmup or fine-tuning stages. Pristine Real impressions serve as the enrolled Gallery; distorted/altered impressions serve as unseen Probes.

3. CLAHE Preprocessing

Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) normalizes non-uniform illumination and skin dryness artifacts. Enhanced single-channel ridges are duplicated into 3 channels to maintain full compatibility with ImageNet pretrained CNN filters.
